# The Reel Contender knowledge base

Source: https://thereelcontender.com/

## About

The Reel Contender is a private sport fishing charter service based out of
Panama City Beach, Florida. It is owned and operated by David and Kim Burke.
David is a USCG licensed captain and personally guides every trip. He was
born in Niceville, FL, grew up in Bruce, FL (about 15 minutes from Panama
City), and has lived in the Florida panhandle for 40 years. He has a deep
knowledge of local fishing regulations and the fish species found off the
coast of Panama City Beach.

The vessel is a 28' Contender center console with twin 200hp Yamaha HPDI
engines, built for comfort and speed on both near shore and offshore Gulf
trips.

Phone: 850-259-2789

## FAQ

**How many anglers per trip?**
The Reel Contender Charter Service offers a more personalized fishing
experience well suited for smaller private fishing parties from 1 to 5
anglers.

**What is provided with my trip?**
Bait, tackle and license will be provided.

**What will I need to bring?**
Sunglasses, sunscreen, drinks, snacks and a cooler to carry your catch home.
Be sure to wear shoes with light non-skid bottoms. It is customary to tip
your deck hand.

**How long will my trip be?**
Trips offered are between 5 to 10 hours and can be customized to fit your
needs.

**What about poor weather?**
Capt. David will gladly answer any questions and if he feels that the trip
will be unsafe or uncomfortable then the trip can be rescheduled for another
date.

**How do I book a trip?**
Call or text 850-259-2789, or use the contact form on the Contact page.
